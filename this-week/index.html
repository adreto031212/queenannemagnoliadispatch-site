What the city decided and what changed

The city is studying whether to repair or replace the W Dravus St Bridge, and the comment window closes Nov. 24

Built in 1935, the bridge links Magnolia, Interbay and Queen Anne over BNSF's Balmer Yard and carries more than 21,000 vehicles a day.

Seattle Christmas Market

Nov 20 to Dec 26 · Nov 20-Dec 13: weekdays 4-9 PM, weekends 11:30 AM-9 PM; longer hours closer to Christmas · Fisher Pavilion and South Fountain Lawn, Seattle Center. An outdoor European-style market with more than 60 vendors, a carousel, mulled wine and Santa photos. Organizer's page

Queen Anne Farmers Market: Thanksgiving Harvest Market

Nov 21 · 10 AM to 3 PM · Queen Anne Ave N at W Crockett St. An extra Saturday market for the Thanksgiving table and food gifts. Organizer's page
