Heads up

  • Coastal flood watch for Seattle's shoreline, through 5 p.m. today. The National Weather Service says flooding is possible on low shoreline ground at this morning's 6:48 a.m. high tide; Friday morning's tide is as high. Stay back from the water's edge at Smith Cove and West Point. More below. NWS Seattle
  • Buses run a Sunday schedule today, the RapidRide D Line on 15th Ave W included, and a regular weekday schedule Friday. Link light rail runs a Sunday schedule today and a Saturday schedule Friday. King County Metro

On the calendar

Seattle Christmas Market

Nov 20 to Dec 26 · Nov 20-Dec 13: weekdays 4-9 PM, weekends 11:30 AM-9 PM; longer hours closer to Christmas · Fisher Pavilion and South Fountain Lawn, Seattle Center. An outdoor European-style market with more than 60 vendors, a carousel, mulled wine and Santa photos. Organizer's page

Seattle Center Winterfest

Nov 27 to Dec 31 · Winter Train & Village 10 AM-6 PM daily; ice carving Saturdays at noon; movies Fridays 6:30 PM · Seattle Center (Armory and Mural Amphitheatre). A 50-year-old model train village in the Armory, Saturday ice carving, weekend stage shows and free Friday holiday movies. Organizer's page

Queen Anne Grand Tree Lighting

Dec 4 · Time not posted yet · Upper Queen Anne Ave N (the 2024 lighting was at the Towne Square, 1920 Queen Anne Ave N). The Queen Anne Chamber lights the trees along the Avenue. Organizer's page

Magnolia Winterfest 2026

Dec 5 · 2 to 8 PM; farmers holiday market 10 AM-2 PM · Magnolia Village, W McGraw St. Magnolia's holiday day: free Santa photos, the community tree lighting, the Jingle Bell Car Show, carolers, Ballard High bands, a Toys for Tots drive at the fire station and shops open until 8. Organizer's page

Magnolia Holiday Art Market

Dec 5 · 1 to 5 PM · Church of the Ascension, 2330 Viewmont Way W. Local artists sell their work with music and treats on Winterfest afternoon. Organizer's page
