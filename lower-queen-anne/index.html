The latest in Lower Queen Anne / Uptown

November 12, 2026

Seattle Center's Winterfest Train & Village opens Friday, Nov. 27, in the Armory's South Court. The model train and village display is free and runs 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily through Dec. 31 (until 8 p.m. on opening day, 4 p.m. on Dec. 24, closed Dec. 25); a turn at the controls has a $2 suggested donation. Seattle Center

October 29, 2026

Counterbalance Park, 700 Queen Anne Avenue North at Roy Street, is a level 12,000-square-foot park named for the former trolley that ran there. Open 6 AM to 10 PM. Seattle Parks

October 22, 2026

The Día de Muertos Festival fills the Armory Food & Event Hall on Saturday, October 31, and Sunday, November 1. It is free, part of Seattle Center's Festál series, with altars, performances, face painting and sugar skull workshops. This weekend, October 24 and 25, the Seattle Antiquarian Book Fair is in the Exhibition Hall. Día de Muertos · Seattle Center

October 17, 2026

The new Memorial Stadium topped out this year and is due in fall 2027. Watch it rise from the viewing windows on Harrison Street next to Artists at Play and on 3rd Avenue across from the International Fountain. Around it this week: the Northwest Record Show at the Armory on Sunday, October 18, Seattle Opera's Salome from October 17 to 31, and the Light the Night Walk on Saturday, October 24. Memorial Stadium · Seattle Center

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Queen Anne & Magnolia newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Queen Anne & Magnolia that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Queen Anne & Magnolia's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
