Fri Nov 20 – Sat Dec 26 · Nov 20-Dec 13: weekdays 4-9 PM, weekends 11:30 AM-9 PM; longer hours closer to Christmas

Seattle Christmas Market

An outdoor European-style market with more than 60 vendors, a carousel, mulled wine and Santa photos.

Fisher Pavilion and South Fountain Lawn, Seattle Center · Timed-entry tickets; kids 6 and under free; family season pass $64.99 · Organizer's page

Sat Nov 21 · 10 AM to 3 PM

Queen Anne Farmers Market: Thanksgiving Harvest Market

An extra Saturday market for the Thanksgiving table and food gifts.

Queen Anne Ave N at W Crockett St · Free to browse · Organizer's page

Sat Nov 21 · 10 AM to 2 PM

Magnolia Farmers Market: Harvest Market

The market returns for Thanksgiving shopping with live music, cooking demos and free face painting.

Magnolia Village, W McGraw St · Free to browse · Organizer's page

Fri Nov 27 – Thu Dec 31 · Winter Train & Village 10 AM-6 PM daily; ice carving Saturdays at noon; movies Fridays 6:30 PM

Seattle Center Winterfest

A 50-year-old model train village in the Armory, Saturday ice carving, weekend stage shows and free Friday holiday movies.

Seattle Center (Armory and Mural Amphitheatre) · Free; the train takes donations · Organizer's page

Fri Dec 4 · Time not posted yet

Queen Anne Grand Tree Lighting

The Queen Anne Chamber lights the trees along the Avenue.

Upper Queen Anne Ave N (the 2024 lighting was at the Towne Square, 1920 Queen Anne Ave N) · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 2 to 8 PM; farmers holiday market 10 AM-2 PM

Magnolia Winterfest 2026

Magnolia's holiday day: free Santa photos, the community tree lighting, the Jingle Bell Car Show, carolers, Ballard High bands, a Toys for Tots drive at the fire station and shops open until 8.

Magnolia Village, W McGraw St · Free; Santa photos free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 1 to 5 PM

Magnolia Holiday Art Market

Local artists sell their work with music and treats on Winterfest afternoon.

Church of the Ascension, 2330 Viewmont Way W · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 12 – Sun Dec 13 · Sat 7:30 PM; Sun 2 PM

Magnolia Chorale: Daring to Dream

The chorale's December concerts, with the Northwest Girlchoir and Ola Gjeilo's Dreamweaver.

Magnolia Lutheran Church, 2414 31st Ave W · Tickets through Magnolia Chorale · Organizer's page

Thu Dec 31 · Armory music 8 PM to midnight; fountain show 9 PM

New Year's Eve at Seattle Center

Live music in the Armory, a Fountain of Light show at 9 and a view of the Space Needle's midnight show.

Armory and International Fountain, Seattle Center · Free · Organizer's page
