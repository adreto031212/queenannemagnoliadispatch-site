Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Queen Anne & Magnolia newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Queen Anne & Magnolia that is news too.

Why does a brokerage publish it?

RexMont Real Estate has worked in these neighborhoods for years and nobody was writing about them at the neighborhood level. The newsletter is free. If you ever want to talk about selling or buying, you will know where to find us. That is the whole arrangement.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today, inside your neighborhood block. It is never sold, shared, or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
