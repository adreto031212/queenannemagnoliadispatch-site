The latest in Interbay

October 29, 2026

The Interbay Athletic Complex, 3027 17th Avenue West, was built with $8.6 million in private funding through a partnership of Seattle Parks, Interbay Golf Centers and Seattle Pacific University. It holds a nine-hole, par-28 golf course, an 80-station driving range, an 18-hole putting course, a 900-seat soccer stadium, ballfields and a P-Patch. In 2024 it finished an accessibility project that cleared 126 items on the city's barrier-removal list. Seattle Parks

October 22, 2026

Fishermen's Terminal on Salmon Bay was the Port of Seattle's first construction project: pile drivers started on February 15, 1913, and the terminal was dedicated on January 10, 1914, with moorage for 100 boats. The Seattle Fishermen's Memorial there carries the names of more than 500 local commercial fishermen and women lost at sea. Port of Seattle · Memorial

October 17, 2026

The Port is designing more shore-power connections at Pier 91, due online in 2027, so more cruise ships at the Smith Cove terminal can plug in at berth. (evergreen) Next door, Smith Cove Park runs a 0.7-mile path past the warehouses to a promenade with picnic tables and a view down Elliott Bay to Alki. Port of Seattle · Smith Cove Park

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Queen Anne & Magnolia newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Queen Anne & Magnolia that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Queen Anne & Magnolia's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
