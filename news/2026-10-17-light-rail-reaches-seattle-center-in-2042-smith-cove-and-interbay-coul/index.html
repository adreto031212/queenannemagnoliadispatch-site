Light rail reaches Seattle Center in 2042; Smith Cove and Interbay could wait until about 2060

October 17, 2026 · Queen Anne & Magnolia Dispatch, issue 1 · All Queen Anne & Magnolia news

Sound Transit's updated plan funds the line to Seattle Center. The Smith Cove, Interbay and Ballard stations make 2042 only if the agency finds about $1 billion in savings or financial capacity by 2030 and the rest of the money by 2033. Board members and the mayor take questions Monday, October 19, 6 to 8 PM, at the National Nordic Museum, 2655 Market Street. Sound Transit · Forum · City Council
