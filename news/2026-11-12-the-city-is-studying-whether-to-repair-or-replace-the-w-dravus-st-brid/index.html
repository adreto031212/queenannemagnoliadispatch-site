The city is studying whether to repair or replace the W Dravus St Bridge, and the comment window closes Nov. 24

November 12, 2026 · Queen Anne & Magnolia Dispatch, issue 5 · All Queen Anne & Magnolia news

Built in 1935, the bridge links Magnolia, Interbay and Queen Anne over BNSF's Balmer Yard and carries more than 21,000 vehicles a day. SDOT says it is open and in service, no option has been picked, and a preferred alternative comes in spring 2027. The online open house runs Nov. 10 to 24; the Zoom meeting is Nov. 18. SDOT
